## Research Goal

Understand what "HR Compliance Management" software really is in the market: what obligations it manages, what objects and workflows it is built around, who operates it, and where its boundaries lie against neighboring Types (HRIS, GRC/Compliance Management, Regulatory Change Management, Policy Management, Corporate LMS, Leave Management, transactional compliance niches).

## Initial Boundary (hypothesis before research)

- Core purpose: help an employer know which employment-law obligations apply to it, act on them, and keep proof.
- Users: HR generalists/managers (SMB), HR compliance officers, HR/benefits admins.
- Neighbors: HRIS (employee records), Compliance Management Platform / GRC (§11), Regulatory Change Management (§11), Policy Management (§10/§11), Corporate LMS (§09), Leave & Absence Management (§09), Background Check / I-9 platforms (§09), Payroll (§09).
- Unknowns: Is this a real distinct category or an umbrella of unrelated niches? Is vendor-curated legal content definitional? What are the product poles?

## Research Questions

1. What obligations do these products manage (postings, training, policies/handbooks, wage & hour, leave, safety, recordkeeping, reporting)?
2. What are the core objects (requirement/obligation, jurisdiction, employer profile, alert, task, training assignment, policy, evidence)?
3. What is the canonical workflow (law change → applicability → alert → action → evidence → report)?
4. Who uses it, at what scale, through which channels (direct, broker/PEO, dealer, suite)?
5. Where are the boundaries vs GRC, RCM, LMS, HRIS, leave/payroll execution systems?

### Layer B findings (cross-product commonality)

1. **Employer as the compliance subject** — all four sell to employers; none is employee-facing.
2. **Employment-law obligation knowledge as supplied content** — every product operates a legal/research content engine (in-house legal team, research team, monitored-law database, legally-vetted course library) and turns it into employer-consumable guidance. Form varies (alerts, summaries, posters, courses); the supply mechanism is universal in the sample.
3. **Applicability matching to the employer's situation** — every product's central value step is "what applies to YOU" (business profile, locations, roster, state).
4. **Fulfillment machinery** — every product drives or performs the required action (to-dos, handbook updates, training delivery, poster shipping, recordkeeping products). None stops at information.
5. **Retained evidence / audit-readiness** — every product frames proof as a first-class output (audit-ready records, audit reports, completion reporting, recordkeeping).
6. **Continuous law-change monitoring with notifications** — universal; the loop is explicitly recurring.
7. **Multi-jurisdiction handling** — federal/state/local layering is the shared geographic model (US-centric sample; GovDocs extends to Canada).

### Layer C (canonical inference)

The Type is best modeled as an **employer-side compliance loop**: applicability-matched employment-obligation knowledge → fulfillment → retained evidence, run continuously against a changing law landscape and a changing employer profile. The obligation register bound to the employer's applicability profile is the center; content supply, training, posters, experts, and dashboards are realizations of its legs.

## Abstraction Hierarchy

### L0 — Defining Invariant (minimal)

1. **Employer-side operation** — the organization (not the employee) is the compliance subject.
2. **Employment-related legal obligations as the managed object** — requirements that attach to an organization because it employs people (wage & hour, postings, mandated training, leave, safety, recordkeeping, classification, benefits-related mandates, termination rules).
3. **Applicability binding** — obligations matched to the employer's specific situation (jurisdictions, locations, size, industry, worker types); "what applies to us" is the pivotal record.
4. **Fulfillment machinery** — the system drives or performs the actions that discharge obligations (update policy, assign/deliver training, post notices, adjust pay practices, retain records).
5. **Retained compliance evidence** — completion records, audit trails, versioned documents, reports producible on demand.

Remove #1 → employee-facing training/HR app. Remove #2/#3 → generic law library or regulatory news feed. Remove #4 → content subscription only. Remove #5 → compliance assistance without management record. Remove the employment domain → generic GRC.

### L1 — Common Mature Structure

- Continuous law-change monitoring + notifications (email + in-platform)
- Compliance dashboard / status view with action prioritization
- To-do / task tracking with completion states
- Vendor-curated guidance library (jurisdiction-filtered, plain-language)
- Mandated-training assignment & completion tracking
- Policy / employee-handbook management with law-linked update prompts
- Multi-jurisdiction support (federal/state/local; multi-state, multi-location)
- Human expert/advisor access
- Audit/reporting surfaces (status reports, audit-ready exports)
- HRIS/payroll integration for employee/location data

### L2 — Variant / Optional

- Physical fulfillment logistics (poster printing/shipping) vs digital-only
- Location-level geocoding precision (rooftop vs ZIP)
- Breadth: single-domain specialist (training-only, postings-only) vs multi-domain platform
- Distribution channel: direct, broker/PEO-embedded, dealer/retail, content licensing/white-label
- Customer tier: one-person-HR SMB vs enterprise multi-location
- Adjacent HR tools (job descriptions, salary benchmarking, benefits documents)
- Language/regional programs (Spanish, Canada)
- AI assistance (law-tracking copilots, course authoring, Q&A)
- Delivery of training content itself vs linking out to LMS

### L3 — Vendor-specific (research notes only)

- Mineral: Mineral Intelligence™ / Mineral Elite / Mineral Platform™ naming; "3,000+ regulations" and "1.7M+ employers" claims; "18 years average expert experience" claim; ARIES AI assistant; side-by-side handbook text comparison + one-click updates; Take Action / Plan Ahead dashboard framing.

## Boundary Findings

| Neighboring Type | Relationship | Distinction (what to remove to cross the boundary) |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance Management Platform / GRC (§11) | adjacent, cross-domain | GRC manages enterprise-wide obligations/controls/audits for a compliance function; HR compliance is domain-scoped (employment obligations) and HR-operated. Remove the employment domain + HR operator → GRC. |
| Regulatory Change Management (§11) | adjacent, upstream | RCM's unit is the regulatory change → obligation → response plan, cross-domain; HR compliance's unit is the employer's ongoing compliance state with fulfillment + evidence. Keep only the change feed → RCM/law-alert service. |
| HRIS / Employee Record System (§09) | adjacent, data supplier | HRIS is the employee-data system of record; HR compliance consumes locations/roles/roster and produces compliance states. Remove the obligation object → HRIS. |
| Corporate LMS / Employee Learning (§09) | overlapping pole | LMS delivers/tracks learning generally; compliance training is mandate-driven (jurisdiction rules decide who must be trained, tracked as evidence). EasyLlama straddles the seam. Remove mandate/applicability machinery → LMS. |
| Policy Management (§10/§11) | adjacent, one fulfillment surface | Policy management governs org-wide policy lifecycle; handbook/policy upkeep is one leg of HR compliance. Keep only the policy lifecycle → Policy Management. |
| Leave & Absence / Payroll / Time & Attendance (§09) | adjacent, execution systems | Those execute transactions (leave, pay, time); HR compliance supplies the requirement knowledge that governs them (which leave laws apply, wage floors, thresholds). GovDocs sells requirement data, not administration. Keep the transaction → the execution Type. |
| Background Check / I-9 Compliance (§09) | transactional niche | Verification events for individual hires vs continuous obligation management. Mineral sells I-9 compliance as a separate product line — confirming the seam. |
| HR Case Management / Employee Relations (§09) | adjacent | Case management handles individual employee issues; HR compliance manages obligation fulfillment organization-wide. |
| PEO / HR outsourcing | service boundary | Outsourced HR compliance is a service; the software Type remains the tool. Several products are distributed through brokers/PEOs without becoming the service. |

Umbrella assessment: the leaf is one Type with strong product poles (intelligence platform / postings-data specialist / training specialist / catalog vendor). All poles satisfy the L0 loop; none of the poles' distinctive capabilities is shared by all.

## Final Synthesis

HR Compliance Management is the employer-side application that runs the employment-compliance loop: it maintains knowledge of employment-related legal obligations matched to what actually applies to the organization (its jurisdictions, locations, size, workforce), turns changes and gaps into concrete fulfillment actions (policy/handbook updates, mandated training, required postings/notices, pay-practice adjustments, recordkeeping), and retains the evidence that proves compliance on demand. Vendor-curated legal content is the dominant supply mechanism; dashboards, to-dos, training engines, poster logistics, and expert access are the common machinery around the loop. The Type is distinct from GRC (domain + operator), from regulatory news/alert services (fulfillment + evidence), from LMS (mandate-driven, not learning-driven), and from HRIS/leave/payroll execution systems (obligation knowledge vs transaction execution).
