# Research Notes — Corporate Governance Platform

## Research Goal

Resolve the directory question deferred by the §10 pass: is "Corporate Governance Platform" (§11, Legal, Risk, Compliance & Governance) a distinct Application Type, or a duplicate/alias of "Board / Corporate Governance Platform" (§10, Enterprise Operations & Administration)? If distinct, what is its center of gravity; if not, document the Type from the §11 side and record the alias finding for joint review.

The §10 pass (2026-09-06) documented the board-meeting-cycle-centered Type and flagged: "probable duplicate/alias leaves — market products conflate board management with broader corporate-governance suites (Diligent One sells board management as the entry point to entities/risk/compliance; OnBoard brands itself 'governance system of record') — recommend joint review for merge or an explicit center-of-gravity split when the §11 leaf is processed."

## Initial Boundary (hypothesis before research)

Three competing hypotheses:

1. **Alias hypothesis**: "Corporate governance platform" is enterprise-suite branding of board management software; the population is the same as §10; the L0 is the same.
2. **Governance-program hypothesis**: §11 has a distinct center of gravity — the organization's standing governance structure (legal entities, boards/committees, officers/directors with terms) plus recurring governance obligations (D&O questionnaires, board evaluations, conflict disclosures, filings, governance calendar) as a managed system, with the meeting cycle as one governed activity among several.
3. **Label-collision hypothesis**: "governance" vocabulary is used by three different product families — board meeting software, entity management (corporate secretarial) software, and GRC platforms — and the §11 leaf is the collision point of all three labels.

Likely confusions to resolve: Legal Entity Management (§10), Entity Compliance Management (§11), Governance Risk & Compliance Platform (§11), Committee / Board Management (§25, already processed as the association-market instance of §10).

## Research Questions

1. What do products that self-identify as a "corporate governance platform" actually center on — the meeting cycle, the governance structure, or the obligations program?
2. Is the governance-program layer (entities, officers/terms, questionnaires, evaluations, conflicts) ever the defining center of a standalone product, or only a module set around a meeting-cycle core?
3. Where does the entity-management pole (corporate secretarial / "Governance Ops" software) belong — this Type or Legal Entity Management?
4. Where is the seam against GRC platforms (risk/compliance/audit programs)?
5. Does the historical check hold: would paper-era governance practice (minute books, company registers, paper D&O questionnaires, consultant-run board evaluations) satisfy the proposed defining core?

## Canonical Model (abstraction hierarchy)

### L0 — Defining Invariant

The researched evidence supports the **same L0 as the §10 sibling leaf** — the products sold under the "corporate governance platform" label center on the governed-body meeting cycle:

```text
Governed body (board or committee) with an identified member roster
└── Meeting as the unit of governance work (dated occasion of a specific body)
    ├── Agenda structuring the meeting
    ├── Board pack: meeting materials assembled against the agenda
    │   and distributed to the body's members under member-scoped confidential access
    └── Governance record: minutes with recorded decisions/resolutions/actions,
        confirmed/approved and retained as the body's official record
```

Justification:

- All three board-facing samples (Diligent, Nasdaq, OnBoard) make the meeting cycle the flagship; the governance-program layer is sold as modules around it.
- No sampled product is a "corporate governance platform" without the meeting cycle. Athennian — the only "governance"-branded sample without one — self-categorizes as entity management.
- The remove test: remove the meeting cycle from Diligent One's governance category and what remains (Entities + Market Intelligence + conflicts/policy modules) is entity management plus governance data — the Legal Entity Management leaf's population, not a new Type.

### L1 — Common Mature Structure

Two families of standard capability:

**The meeting-cycle machinery** (documented in depth by the §10 sibling pass): agenda builder, pack assembly/versioning, director reader with annotations and read receipts, minutes workflow with confirmation and signing, voting/resolutions/actions, governance repository, notices/calendar/video, security baseline, multi-body support, AI assistance.

**The governance-program layer** — the layer the §11 label emphasizes, present as modules in every sampled board-facing product:

- **Entity & subsidiary governance** — a centralized corporate record per legal entity (registration, jurisdiction, officers/directors, ownership), structure charts, compliance deadlines and tasks, minute books and governance documents (Diligent Entities; Nasdaq Entity Management integration; Athennian as the standalone pole).
- **Governance people data** — directors/officers as governed records with roles, classes, terms; skills tracking; diversity reporting (OnBoard Roles & Terms/Skills/Diversity; entity modules' officer records).
- **Governance questionnaires** — annually recurring per-person collection campaigns: D&O questionnaires (directors/officers disclose interests, independence, conflicts for disclosure and regulatory use) and compliance questionnaires (Nasdaq EnGauge; Diligent questionnaires; OnBoard D&O questionnaires).
- **Board evaluations & assessments** — structured, questionnaire-driven evaluation cycles over the board, committees, individual directors, and management, producing aggregate insights (Nasdaq Board Evaluations & CEO/Management Evaluations; OnBoard Board Assessments; Convene Assure per §10 pass).
- **Conflict-of-interest machinery** — disclosure collection and review (Diligent Conflict of Interest Manager; interest registers in BoardPro per §10 pass).
- **Governance calendar** — the annual rhythm that sequences meetings, questionnaires, evaluations, disclosures, and filing deadlines (Nasdaq lifecycle packaging; entity-module deadline tracking; annual work plans per §10 pass).
- **Governance intelligence & education** (suite pole) — market/shareholder/executive-compensation data feeds, board education and certification (Diligent Market Intelligence, Board Education; Nasdaq Center for Board Excellence).

### L2 — Variant / Optional Structure

- **Packaging poles**: pure-play board portal (meeting cycle only) → governance suite (meeting cycle + program layer) → GRC umbrella (governance + risk + compliance + audit). The same vendor can span all three (Diligent One).
- **Suite-member independence**: suite products are sold independently (Nasdaq FAQ: each product functions independently; start with any).
- **Lifecycle/industry packaging**: pre-IPO/private/public programs; industry editions (banking, insurance, healthcare, higher ed, PE, law firms, fund administration, nonprofit).
- **Public-sector variant**: transparency sites, livestreaming, accessibility (Diligent Community — §10 pass).
- **Association/nonprofit audience variant**: Committee / Board Management (§25 pass).
- **Entity-management-led packaging**: "Governance Ops" branding of entity management (Athennian) — adjacent Type, not this one.
- **Corporate service provider model**: one platform administering many client organizations' governance (Diligent CSP positioning).
- **Regional/deployment**: data residency, national eID integrations (§10 pass).

## Boundary Findings

1. **vs Board / Corporate Governance Platform (§10) — ALIAS CONFIRMED from the §11 side.** The market label "corporate governance platform" names the same software category as board management software. Evidence: (a) the product population is the same (Diligent, Nasdaq, OnBoard appear under both labels); (b) the defining core of every sampled "governance platform" is the §10 meeting-cycle core; (c) the governance-program layer is sold as modules around that core, never as a meeting-free standalone platform under this label. Candidate outcomes for joint review: merge the leaves, or keep both with an explicit center-of-gravity split (§10 = meeting-cycle-centered pure-play pole; §11 = governance-program-centered suite pole). This document records the finding; the taxonomy decision belongs to joint review.
2. **vs Legal Entity Management (§10)** — The entity-management family (Diligent Entities standalone, Athennian, and peers) centers the legal entity record: registrations, officers, share/equity structures, filings, minute books. It uses governance vocabulary ("Governance Ops") but has no governed-body meeting cycle. Remove the meeting cycle from this Type and the remainder is entity management — the seam is the governed body. Entity management appears inside governance suites as the entity-governance module.
3. **vs Entity Compliance Management (§11)** — Entity-level obligation tracking (filings, renewals, registered agents) as the center vs the governance function's broader program (bodies + people + obligations + meetings) here. Adjacent; the entity module's deadline machinery feeds it.
4. **vs Governance Risk & Compliance Platform (§11)** — GRC platforms center enterprise risk, compliance, and audit programs. Diligent One spans both under one brand: its Governance category is this Type; its Risk/Compliance/Audit categories are GRC. The seam: governed-body governance work vs enterprise risk/compliance programs. The "corporate governance platform" label does not extend to GRC even when one vendor sells both.
5. **vs Committee / Board Management (§25)** — Association-market audience variant of the same Type (confirmed by the §25 pass). Unchanged here.
6. **vs Legislative Management System / Government Meeting & Agenda Management (§24)** — Public-sector board portals sit between the Types (§10 finding, unchanged).
7. **Degradation tests**: remove the meeting cycle → entity management + governance data (Legal Entity Management territory); remove the governance-program layer → pure board portal (§10 pole); remove the governed body → GRC/document management; remove the record → collaboration tooling.

## Historical / Market-Sample Check

- Paper-era governance practice satisfies the L0: a corporate secretary maintaining the minute book, company register, and board papers; annual D&O questionnaires collected on paper; board evaluations run by consultants — the governed body, its meetings, its member-scoped papers, and its confirmed record all predate software. The governance-program layer is a digitization of long-standing governance practice (questionnaires, evaluations, registers), not a modern invention — so the layer's presence in the standard-capability set does not overfit to the current market.
- Regional products (ANZ board portals, Asian board portals, UK company-secretarial software) fit the same core; the entity-management pole is strongest in common-law corporate-secretarial traditions but is a separate family.
- The check passes: the L0 does not depend on any current-era implementation (AI, cloud, suite packaging).

## Uncertainties

- No Tier-1 operational documentation was fetched in this pass; the governance-program modules' workflow mechanics (questionnaire campaign lifecycle, evaluation scoring, entity-record change control) are described at capability level only.
- Nasdaq EnGauge's product-vs-technology status is unverified.
- OnBoard's governance-pillar detail is carried from the §10 pass's homepage navigation (positioning level) and was not re-verified.
- Whether mid-market pure-play portals commonly bundle entity management was not researched; the observation that entity modules appear at the enterprise pole (Diligent, Nasdaq) is sample-bounded.
- The merge-vs-split taxonomy decision is explicitly left to joint review; this pass documents the Type from the §11 side and records the finding.

## Final Synthesis

"Corporate Governance Platform" resolves to the same Application Type as "Board / Corporate Governance Platform": the board-governance software category whose defining core is the governed body with its member roster, the meeting as the unit of governance work, the agenda-structured pack distributed under member-scoped confidential access, and the confirmed governance record retained as the official record. What the §11 label adds is the governance program around that core — entity and subsidiary governance, governance people data (roles and terms), recurring governance obligations (D&O and compliance questionnaires, board evaluations, conflict-of-interest disclosures), and the governance calendar that sequences them — packaged at the enterprise pole as the governance suite, and at the widest pole merged into a GRC umbrella. The label collides with two sibling families that also use "governance" vocabulary: entity management (which centers the legal entity record and lacks the meeting cycle) and GRC platforms (which center risk/compliance/audit programs). The alias finding against the §10 leaf is confirmed and recorded for joint review.
