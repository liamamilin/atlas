## Abstraction Layers

### L0 — Defining Invariant

Identical to the sibling's ratified core (independently re-derived this pass from ESG-labeled products):

1. **The organization's multi-domain ESG data estate of record** — quantitative metrics and qualitative records spanning environmental, social, and governance domains, scoped to the organization's structure (entities/sites/locations) and to periods, held as a managed, auditable record. Remove → point tools (carbon accounting = emissions only; ECM = energy; EHS = operational records) or a generic data warehouse.
2. **The organization-wide collection operation keeping the estate current** — structured acquisition from internal contributors and value-chain parties (intake workflows, forms/surveys, integrations, supplier-facing collection) with validation and approval before data enters the record. Remove → static warehouse or report generator with nothing feeding it.
3. **The ESG program operated over the estate** — targets/goals, initiatives, and progress tracking feeding internal decisions and external outputs, continuously rather than only at reporting time. Remove → ESG data management (the esg-reporting seam) or generic goal tracking.

Jointly-held load-bearing (inherited from the sibling's analysis and consistent with this sample): 1 alone = ESG data warehouse; 2 alone = survey/form tooling; 3 alone = generic goal tracker; 1+2 without 3 = ESG data management; 1+3 without 2 = stale scorecard; 2+3 without 1 = program machinery over nothing.

Binding: the organization's own ESG/sustainability performance as the subject (remove → generic performance management).

### L1 — Common Mature Structure

Standard capabilities across the sampled population, not definitional:

- framework/standards libraries and disclosure generation (collect-once-report-many)

- organizational modeling (entities, sites, subsidiaries, portfolio companies) and roll-ups
- data-quality machinery (validation rules, anomaly flagging, estimates)

- benchmarking (peer/sector comparison)

## Rejected Findings (candidate boundaries tested and rejected)

1. **E/S/G three-pillar organization as the defining structure.** Rejected: the pillar enumeration is a vocabulary for the same multi-domain estate the sibling pass documented ("sustainability" estates also span E+S+G — the sibling's core says so explicitly). Position Green's FAQ uses E/S/G to enumerate data types; the sibling's sampled products organize the same content as "sustainability metrics." No machinery difference observed.
2. **Ratings/questionnaire machinery as the center.** Rejected: present across the sample but as an output channel of the estate (collect-once-report-many), matching the sibling's "outputs" leg. Products centered on questionnaire production would drift toward esg-reporting-platform, not a distinct ESG-management Type.
3. **"Investor-grade" data posture as the center.** Rejected: it is the ESG label's characteristic *audience emphasis* (investors, raters, regulators) rather than a structure. The sibling's sample shows sustainability-labeled products making the same investor-facing claims (Sweep's investor framing; Envizi's audit-grade data). Posture, not boundary.
4. **A distinct "management object" (programs/initiatives as first-class beyond targets).** Rejected as a boundary: the sibling's core already holds "targets/goals + initiatives/strategy + progress tracking" as the program leg. This pass found no additional managed object in the ESG-labeled sample that the sibling's core lacks.
5. **"ESG" as a narrower scope than "sustainability"** (e.g., ESG = investor-facing subset, sustainability = broader operations). Rejected on evidence: the same products carry both labels for the same scope (Novisto, Position Green), and the ESG-labeled sample includes operational-data capture at sites (Benchmark Gensuite) — the full scope, not a subset.

## Boundary Findings

**Primary verdict — ALIAS.** "ESG Management Platform" and "Sustainability Management Platform" name one product population and one defining core. Evidence:

- Self-applied double labeling: Novisto's Manage page is titled "Enterprise ESG Management Software" and closes with "Explore the sustainability management software…" — both labels, one product, one page (first-hand this pass). Position Green's data-management page says "our ESG management platform" under the title "Sustainability software for stress-free data management" (first-hand this pass).
- Vendor-defined category: Benchmark Gensuite's own suite page defines "sustainability management software" in exactly the estate+collection+program terms, while its analyst recognition is "ESG & Sustainability Software" (first-hand this pass).
- Analyst category naming fuses the labels ("ESG & Sustainability Reporting Software", "ESG & Sustainability Software" — Verdantix badges displayed by sampled vendors).
- Structural identity: every leg of the sibling's ratified core is present in every ESG-labeled product sampled; no distinguishing structure survived testing (see Rejected Findings).
- Historical continuity: the ESG acronym is era-vocabulary (investor/rating-driven, 2000s onward) for a program population that predates the label (CSR-era spreadsheet programs satisfy the core — sibling's historical check; EHS-suite-carried sustainability modules such as Sphera's and Benchmark's families long predate the ESG label boom and carry the same core).

Per the greenhouse-gas-accounting/carbon-accounting precedent: keep both leaves documented and cross-referenced, this document written from the ESG angle; consolidation recommended at a taxonomy pass.

**Seams held consistent from this side (all processed siblings):**

- vs **esg-reporting-platform**: center-of-gravity seam ratified by two prior passes and re-confirmed — management center = ongoing estate/collection/program operation; reporting center = disclosure production starting from what must be disclosed. Every product sampled here also produces disclosures (overlap zone wide; center of gravity decides per product).
- vs **esg-disclosure-management**: none of this pass's sampled products shows filing-grade machinery (tagged/XBRL, regulator submission) on observed pages; the filer pole remains the disclosure-management leaf's territory. Watch item inherited: tagging mandates may pull ESG-native platforms toward the filing pole (unverified).
- vs **carbon-accounting-platform**: embedded carbon engine is a capability of the estate in all sampled products (Novisto Scope animations, Position Green carbon use case, Benchmark automated carbon accounting, Sphera Environmental Accounting family) — consistent with both prior passes' keep-both.
- vs **supplier-sustainability-management**: supplier data appears as collected input (surveys, portals) in the sample; managing suppliers' own sustainability standing across the boundary is the sibling Type.
- vs **ehs-hse-platform**: the two EHS-carried samples (Sphera, Benchmark Gensuite) demonstrate the seam from the carrier side — operational EHS machinery (incidents, permits, safety) is suite context; the ESG-management core is the performance estate + program. Quentic's module split (recorded by the sibling pass) matches.
- vs **environmental-management-system**: no ISO-cycle machinery (aspects register, management-system cycle) observed in this sample; distinct centers, consistent with the sibling pass's candidate seam.

## Uncertainties

1. No help-center/operational documentation reached this pass (Sphera product subpage 404; others marketing-level). All claims are capability-level; no step-level workflows, numeric limits, or defaults asserted.
2. Whether any product population answers to "ESG management" but explicitly rejects "sustainability management" (or vice versa) — none found in this sample or the sibling's; absence of counter-evidence is not proof, but the double-labeling evidence is strong.
3. The direction of label drift over time (ESG vocabulary has faced political pushback in some markets; vendors may re-brand toward "sustainability") — not researched this pass; recorded as a watch item only.
4. Sphera's Corporate Sustainability sub-products were observed only through the home page's navigation and family descriptions; the "Sustainability Performance Management Software" product page itself was not reached (404). Its exact capability set is inferred from family naming only — held at low strength.
5. Filing-grade depth in the sampled products (whether any ships XBRL machinery behind the marketing layer) — unverified; the seam vs esg-disclosure-management is drawn on observed-page evidence only.

## Final Synthesis

The joint review is discharged: **ESG Management Platform is an ALIAS of Sustainability Management Platform.** The market's "ESG management" label and "sustainability management" label name one product population with one defining core — the organization's multi-domain ESG data estate of record, the organization-wide collection operation that keeps it current, and the ESG program (targets, initiatives, progress) operated over the estate. The ESG label contributes audience emphasis (investors, raters, regulators) and the E/S/G pillar vocabulary, not structure. Both directory leaves stand, cross-referenced; this document is written from the ESG angle; consolidation is recommended at a taxonomy pass.
