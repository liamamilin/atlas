## Initial Boundary (working hypothesis before research)

A Sustainability Management Platform is the sustainability/ESG team's system for operating the organization's own sustainability program: holding the organization's multi-domain ESG data estate, running goals/targets and initiatives, collecting data from across the organization, and producing outputs (reports, disclosures, ratings responses). The center of gravity is the ongoing program/data operation — not the disclosure deliverable (esg-reporting), not the emissions inventory (carbon accounting), not the energy-data engine (ECM), not the resource-consumption record (REM).

## Canonical Abstraction (L0 / L1 / L2 / L3)

### L0 — Defining Invariant (deliberately minimal)

A Sustainability Management Platform is the organization's system for operating its own sustainability program. Three jointly-held structures; remove any one and the product stops being recognizable:

1. **The organization's multi-domain sustainability data estate of record** — quantitative metrics and qualitative records spanning environmental, social, and governance domains, scoped to the organization's own structure (entities/sites/locations) and to periods, held as a managed, auditable system of record. Remove → point tools (carbon accounting = emissions only; ECM = energy; REM = physical resources; EHS = operational records) or a generic data warehouse.
2. **The organization-wide collection operation** — structured acquisition of that data from across the organization and its value chain (intake workflows, forms/surveys, system integrations, contributor roles) with validation and approval keeping the estate current. Remove → a static data warehouse or a report generator with nothing feeding it.
3. **The sustainability program operated over the estate** — targets/goals, initiatives/strategy, and progress tracking that turn the estate into managed performance, feeding internal decisions and external outputs. Remove → ESG data management (the esg-reporting seam) or generic goal tracking.

Jointly-held is load-bearing:

- 1 alone = ESG data warehouse / carbon accounting's data layer.
- 2 alone = survey/form tooling.
- 3 alone = generic goal/project tracker.
- 1+2 without 3 = ESG data management (the seam the esg-reporting pass predicted).
- 1+3 without 2 = stale scorecard nobody refreshes.
- 2+3 without 1 = program machinery over nothing.

Deliberately NOT in L0: cloud delivery, AI, any named framework or regulation (GRI/CSRD/CDP/ISSB...), materiality assessment, carbon engine, XBRL, benchmarking, supplier portals, dashboards, approval workflows as named features.

### Historical / market-sample check (§24)

- Spreadsheet-era sustainability program: a coordinator collects site data into spreadsheets (estate), chases contributors by email (collection operation), tracks CSR targets and initiatives (program loop), produces the annual sustainability report (output). - Pre-"ESG"-label era: "corporate responsibility" / "triple bottom line" programs (late 1990s–2000s) — same structure under different vocabulary. The Type predates its label.

- Anti-overfit guard: "ESG" vocabulary NOT definitional (sustainability/CSR/corporate-responsibility vocabulary satisfies); "carbon" NOT definitional (one domain among several); "annual report cadence" NOT definitional (Novisto's own "beyond the annual report" framing; continuous operation in-sample).

## Final Synthesis

A Sustainability Management Platform is the organization's system for operating its own sustainability program. Its defining core is three jointly-held structures: the organization's multi-domain sustainability data estate of record (E+S+G quantitative metrics and qualitative records, scoped to the org's structure and periods, auditable); the organization-wide collection operation that keeps the estate current (intake workflows, forms/surveys, integrations, contributor roles, validation/approval); and the sustainability program operated over the estate (targets/goals, initiatives/strategy, progress tracking) feeding internal decisions and external outputs. Mature products add framework libraries ("collect once, report to many"), embedded carbon accounting, org-structure modeling, data-quality machinery, audit/assurance support, materiality, benchmarking, BI democratization, and supplier data collection. Variants divide along carrier (pure-play / enterprise-suite / EHS-suite / reporting-adjacent), regulatory posture, segment, and domain depth. The seams hold on the center of gravity: disclosure production (esg-reporting), emissions inventory (carbon accounting), energy-data operations (ECM), resource-consumption record (REM), supplier standing (supplier-sustainability-management), operational EHS records (EHS), material loops (circular economy). The Type predates its "ESG" label: the spreadsheet-era sustainability program satisfies the core. The leaf stands as an independent Type; probable alias with the unprocessed esg-management-platform leaf flagged for joint review.
